Vehicle Dynamics Modelling and Suspension Optimisation using MSC ADAMS and MATLAB

Objective

Developed and validated analytical and simulation models to study handling, stability, and ride dynamics of a performance vehicle. Built single-track and twin-track bicycle models and advanced suspension models (1-DOF, 2-DOF, and 4-DOF) to analyse understeer/oversteer behaviour, weight transfer, and roll–heave optimisation.

Tools Used

  • MSC Adams
  • MATLAB/SIMULINK

Key Highlights

  • Constructed MATLAB-based linear bicycle models to evaluate yaw response and lateral force variation with COG shifts.
  • Built and optimised multi-DOF suspension models in MSC ADAMS to minimise unsprung mass displacement and body roll.
  • Applied design exploration and optimisation tools to tune spring stiffness and damping coefficients for performance-oriented setups.
  • Validated simulation results using Simulink, ensuring correlation between numerical and multibody approaches.
  • Investigated camber effects and damping asymmetry on ride–handling balance, identifying trade-offs between comfort and grip.
